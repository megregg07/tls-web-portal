  <h1 style="color: #1b365d; margin-top: 0; margin-bottom: 25px; font-size: 2.2em;">About the Project</h1>

  <p>
    This project supports the ongoing development of national forensic standards by the Crime Scene Investigation and Reconstruction (CSIR) subcommittee within the Organization of Scientific Area Committees (OSAC) for Forensic Science, in close collaboration with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). The primary focus is the realization of a standardized Interim Performance Assessment (IPA) protocol that enables forensic practitioners and law enforcement agencies to conduct reliable, in-house quality assurance on terrestrial laser scanners (TLSs). This procedure provides an intermediate check to monitor instrument performance between regularly scheduled manufacturer calibrations. 
  </p> <hr style="border: 0; border-top: 1px solid #eaeaea; margin: 30px 0;">

  <h3 style="color: #1b365d; margin-bottom: 15px;">IPA Overview</h3>
  <p>The Interim Performance Assessment (IPA) test procedure consists of two primary components and a supplemental appendix:</p> <ul style="padding-left: 20px; list-style-type: square; margin-bottom: 20px;">
    <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;">
      <strong style="color: #1b365d;">Part I (Accuracy Testing):</strong> Evaluates instrument length accuracy by comparing scanned measurements against reference values.
    </li>
    <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;">
      <strong style="color: #1b365d;">Part II (Precision Assessment):</strong> Analyzes the scanner's overall spherical coordinate precision.
    </li>
    <li style="margin-bottom: 8px;">
      <strong style="color: #1b365d;">Appendix:</strong> Uses an inside-outside test to test for zero error.
    </li>
  </ul>

  <p style="margin-top: 20px;">
    If the test procedure has been performed before, the user may optionally test if the instrument's spherical precision has changed.
  </p>
